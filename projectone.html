Our Capstone Project

The Firefly's Story: When Hands Begin to Glow

A visual storytelling project that celebrates Deaf experiences, Filipino Sign Language, and the beauty of communication through different ways of expressing ourselves.

What Is the Project?

Understanding the idea behind the story.

The Firefly's Story: When Hands Begin to Glow is an educational storytelling project about a young Deaf firefly named Alipat. The story introduces children to visual communication and the importance of understanding and respecting different ways of communicating.

Through illustrations and storytelling, the project shows how communication is not limited to spoken words. It can also happen through gestures, expressions, movements, and sign language.

The project aims to help Deaf and hard-of-hearing children feel represented while encouraging hearing children to understand and appreciate Deaf experiences.

The Story

When Hands Begin to Glow

Alipat is a young Deaf firefly who discovers that communication can happen in different ways. Through light, movement, and visual expression, Alipat learns that being understood does not depend on spoken words.

The story connects Alipat's journey to the experiences of Deaf children and introduces the value of Filipino Sign Language and visual communication.

Project Objectives

The project focuses on three important goals.

01

Representation

Represent Deaf experiences through a meaningful story that allows Deaf and hard-of-hearing children to see themselves in the characters.

02

Awareness

Introduce children to Deaf culture, Filipino Sign Language, and the importance of visual communication.

03

Inclusion

Encourage understanding, respect, and acceptance between Deaf and hearing children.

Project Deliverables

The project brings the story to life through three connected outputs.

Storybook

An illustrated children's story introducing Deaf experiences and visual communication through Alipat's journey.

Digital Flipbook

A digital version of the story that allows readers to explore the illustrated pages using an online format.

Website

An accessible online platform featuring the project, visual learning materials, and Filipino Sign Language resources.

Every Hand Has a Story.

Communication comes in many forms. Through this project, we hope to create a space where Deaf children are represented, visual communication is valued, and everyone feels included.
