Let's Learn Together!

Filipino Sign Language (FSL) is a visual language. It uses hand movements, facial expressions, and body movements to communicate. Explore these five words and practice signing along with the demonstrations.

Five Basic Words

These words are connected to Alipat's story. Watch each FSL demonstration and practice the signs.

WORD 01

Hello

A friendly greeting used when meeting someone.

Watch the sign and practice carefully.

WORD 02

Friend

Someone who cares and shares moments with us.

Watch the sign and practice carefully.

WORD 03

Thank You

A polite expression used to show appreciation or gratitude.

Watch the sign and practice carefully.

WORD 04

Follow

To go after someone or something.

Watch the sign and practice carefully.

WORD 05

Alipat

A Deaf firefly with a glowing light.

Watch the sign and practice carefully.

Remember!

FSL is more than hand movements. Facial expressions and body movements are also important parts of signing. Keep practicing and respect Filipino Sign Language as a language and part of Deaf culture.
